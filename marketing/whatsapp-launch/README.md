## Rebuild or change a status
Edit the `NN-*.html` file (shared look in `status.css`), then `node render.cjs` (or `node render.cjs 03` for one). PNGs land in `png/`.
New tall photos: extend a retouched tray with Codex using the prompt pattern in `docs/product-photo-guide.md` (canvas extension only, tray untouched).

## Posting plan (WhatsApp Status, 24 hours each)
Post 1 and 2 together on launch morning (about 07:30, when people check their phones), 3 at lunch, 4 at 17:00 when dinner is on people's minds, 5 in the evening. Repeat 3 and 5 the next day. Reply to every status reaction personally; most orders will start there.
Ask your regulars and family to reshare status 1 and 3 to their own status, and send status 3 to your broadcast list.
