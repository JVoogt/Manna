## 10. Verification
Google usually asks a new home-based business for **video verification**. Record one continuous clip (no cuts) showing: your street and house number, the branded sticker or label on a tray, the kitchen and the freezer, a meal being packed, and the labelled packaging with the Manna Kitchen name. Keep the phone number on a label in view only if it is the Manna line.
- Approval can take a few days. Do not edit the name or address while it is pending.
- If rejected, fix what the message says and resubmit; do not create a second profile.

## 11. Reviews (this is what moves the ranking)
- Ask every happy customer; send the review link from the profile (Get more reviews, then Share review form). Never offer a discount for a review and never ask only happy customers (that is review gating and is against Google's rules).
- Reply to every review within a day, in your own words.
- Suggested WhatsApp message: *Hi [name], thank you for your order! If you have a moment, a short Google review helps other families find us: [link]. Thank you so much, Cornelia and Edith.*
- Aim for 10 reviews in the first month. That alone beats most of the current top results.
